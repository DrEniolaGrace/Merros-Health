# Pilot plan

Draft to be completed in Phase 5: a 3 month pilot in two Abu Dhabi screening centres, measuring comprehension (Teach Back before/after), time to counselling, counsellor workload, consent patterns and user experience in Arabic and English, with accredited review of all content before launch.

## Partners and referral routes

The directory in Merros is fictional. A pilot would replace it with, and connect to, the genetic counselling institutions listed in Appendix 13 of the Department of Health, Abu Dhabi. *Premarital Screening Standard*. Document reference DoH/STD/PSS/V3, version 3, published September 2024 (revision due September 2027). https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/Feature/Resources/Standards/Premarital-Screening-standard-for-publication.ashx: the hospitals offering genetic counselling and clinical genetics, the assigned services for haemoglobinopathy carriers, and the genetic counselling clinics in maternity, fertility and ambulatory services. Referral rules would follow that appendix. Any such connection needs agreement from those institutions and the Department; merros is an independent prototype. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Health Abu Dhabi; it uses the Department's published standard as a public reference only.

The pilot would also use the standard's own steps as its measures: time from results to results counselling, share of couples counselled together, time to the Premarital Screening and Counselling Report, and referrals made.

The principle behind every screen. Merros never tells anyone whom to marry or what to decide. Success means understanding, timely access to qualified counselling and confident personal choice. A cancelled marriage, or any single personal decision, is never a success measure.

Merros in one minute

Mariam receives her premarital screening result. Merros checks the lab's signature, explains the result in plain Arabic or English and checks her understanding with Teach Back. With her fiancé Khalid she runs a Blind Couple Match on their own phones: only the genes they both carry are revealed, as a shared carrier result followed by their plan. Their genetic counsellor, Dr. Layla, receives a pre-session brief and a Counsellor Copilot that drafts a summary she reviews and signs. A year later, when science reclassifies one of Mariam's variants, Dr. Layla is told first and releases the update after speaking to her. Meanwhile the health system plans counselling from privacy protected statistics, without seeing anyone.

1Overview and the challenge it answers

Abu Dhabi has made premarital genetic screening universal. Testing is solved; understanding and acting on results is not.

The challenge

Fertility Challenge 03 asks for solutions that help people identify, understand and reduce inherited disease risk, in a way that is culturally appropriate and privacy preserving. Genetic results are complex, probabilistic and shared across families. Misunderstood results cause anxiety, stigma, family tension or false certainty. Consanguineous marriage is a long standing practice, so technology must support informed choice without judging anyone. Genetic counsellors are few, and genetic data is among the most private data a person has.

What Merros does

Challenge needHow Merros answers it
Identify inherited riskA signed lab result on the DoH premarital panel (563 genes); Blind Couple Match for shared carrier status; Family Risk Engine for relatives; Risk Beyond the Panel for related couples; Living Results that follow science over time.
UnderstandRisk Explainer in plain language at three reading levels; Teach Back with re-explanation; the Genetic Copilot in Arabic (Gulf dialect) and English, by text or voice; a personal result video.
Reduce"Your plan" after every risk result: counselling, every option with equal weight, one next step. Options Explorer, Values Compass, Find Care, and a care pathway that follows the DoH process.
Culturally appropriateArabic first, right to left; non directive wording enforced by tests (a couple is never "incompatible": they have a "shared carrier result"); options related to personal values, never ranked.
Privacy preservingGranular consent; the couple match runs on the partners' phones; field level encryption; a tamper evident audit log; population statistics with differential privacy.

Two beneficiaries, one loop

The person and the couple understand their result, reach a counsellor sooner, decide by their own values and stay protected as science evolves.

The health system sees where people drop off, where counselling demand is rising and which languages are underserved, without seeing any individual.

2System architecture

One web application, a deterministic risk engine at its centre, and AI only where language is needed, always behind safety checks.

Main parts

  • Web application: Next.js 15 with React 19 and TypeScript, Arabic and English with full right to left layout, designed for phones first. One codebase serves people, counsellors and the programme team, each seeing only their own screens.
  • Risk engine: pure, deterministic code that computes per pregnancy chances with exact fractions (for example 1/4 per shared gene, combined across genes as 1 − (1 − ¼)²). It is the only source of numbers.
  • Copilots: a multi agent pipeline. A router reads the question; a retriever finds approved content; engine tools provide numbers; the model writes the answer; a Cultural and Dialect Adapter renders natural Gulf Arabic; the Safety Verifier checks the draft before anyone sees it.
  • Database: PostgreSQL through Prisma. Results are stored as the lab signed them, encrypted per person; every access is written to a hash chained audit log.
  • Scheduled work: a nightly job checks ClinVar for reclassifications and resets the public demo data.

4Clinical boundaries

Merros explains and connects. It does not diagnose, decide or replace a genetic counsellor.

The AI may

  • Explain a result, a condition or an option in plain language, citing approved content.
  • Use numbers that the risk engine returns through its tools.
  • Answer in Gulf Arabic or English, by text or voice.
  • Draft a patient summary from the counsellor's own notes, for the counsellor to edit and sign.
  • Offer a counsellor whenever a person is the right answer.

The AI may not

  • Calculate or invent any risk number. A number not in the engine's output or approved content is blocked.
  • Tell anyone whom to marry or what to decide, or rank options.
  • Diagnose, or say a baby will or will not have a condition.
  • Use stigmatising or judging language ("incompatible", "defective").
  • Send anything clinical to a patient without a counsellor's signature.

Counsellor sign off

Summaries drafted by the Counsellor Copilot, reclassified results and Risk Beyond the Panel estimates all stay with the counsellor until she reviews them and releases them. The patient sees only what the counsellor signed.

Engine scope

The engine computes autosomal recessive couple risk. X linked, dominant, phase unknown and homozygous findings are routed to a counsellor rather than computed.

5Privacy, security and responsible AI

Consent

Nothing is pre ticked and nothing is shared by default. Each purpose (see my result, compare with my partner, let my counsellor see it, help improve care anonymously, tell me if science changes my result, Risk Beyond the Panel, Values Summary sharing) is a separate, revocable choice with a time limit, visible in Privacy. The right not to know is respected: people who did not ask to be recontacted are never contacted.

Blind Couple Match

Each partner turns their own key on their own phone. Their phones run a private set intersection protocol (elliptic curve, blinded points); the server only relays sealed points and cannot tell which genes the couple shares. Only the genes both carry are revealed, and only once both have agreed. Neither partner sees the rest of the other's result.

Security

  • Signed results: the lab signs each FHIR result (Ed25519). Merros verifies the signature before opening it and refuses a tampered delivery.
  • Encryption: results are encrypted per person (AES-256-GCM with a per person data key wrapped by a master key held as a secret).
  • Audit: every access and change is written to a hash chained, tamper evident audit log that the person can see.
  • Public demo protections: rate limits on the Copilot (20 questions per visitor per hour and a daily cap), a passcode on demo reset, and a nightly data reset.

Population Signals: differential privacy

Every published number has calibrated random noise added (discrete Laplace, ε differential privacy). Each release spends part of a visible quarterly privacy budget (ε 2.0 per release out of ε 6.0); when it is used up, nothing more is published until the next quarter. Any group under 10 people is hidden. Nothing is broken down by tribe, family name or identity group.

Responsible AI

  • Safety Verifier: rules first (unverified numbers, directive, diagnostic and stigmatising wording, missing citation), then a model classifier. A blocked draft is replaced by a safe answer and a counsellor offer.
  • Grounding: answers must cite approved passages; the sources are shown under each answer.
  • Evaluation: a golden set of questions in both languages is run against the Copilot; results are documented.
  • Transparency: every answer is labelled "Education" or "Decision support", and "How this answer was made" shows the agents' steps.
  • Testing: 350+ unit tests cover the risk engine, the couple match protocol, consent, escalation, the Safety Verifier, differential privacy and wording; 30 browser tests cover the main journeys in Arabic and English.

6User testing

A small, early usability test of the prototype, with a simulated scenario and no real patient data.

12people tested Merros
8 → 12 of 12understood their result correctly, first time and after Teach Back
12 of 12would recommend Merros to a friend or relative

Method

Twelve adults used the demo couple (synthetic data) and read Mariam's result as if it were their own, compared it with a partner using the Blind Couple Match, asked the Genetic Copilot questions and completed Teach Back. We recorded whether each person could explain their result correctly in their own words, first without help and then after Teach Back; whether they found the Copilot's answers clear; and whether they would recommend Merros.

MeasureResult
Understood their result correctly the first time8 of 12
Understood their result correctly after Teach Back12 of 12
Found the Copilot's answers clear12 of 12
Would recommend Merros to a friend or relative12 of 12

What this shows, and what it does not

  • Teach Back did its job: the four people who misunderstood at first understood after a second explanation. This supports keeping it as a required step.
  • Twelve people is a small group, and a simulated scenario is not the same as receiving your own result.
  • Participants were not selected to represent the UAE population. These results should not be read as evidence of clinical effect; the pilot (section 9) is designed to measure that.

7Deployment

Today: the live prototype

The prototype runs on Vercel with a Neon PostgreSQL database, at https://merros-health.vercel.app. It deploys automatically from the main branch. Development and all automated tests use a separate database branch; the live site uses its own production branch, and a guard in the code refuses to run seeds or tests against any database that is not explicitly allowed. Secrets live only in the hosting provider's environment settings. Because this hosting is outside the UAE, the live site holds synthetic data only.

Production: Microsoft Azure, UAE North

The production deployment is prepared for Microsoft Azure in the UAE North region, so that genetic data stays in the country. It is defined as infrastructure as code and is ready to switch on:

ComponentRole
Bicep templatesThe whole environment in one file, deployed by a GitHub Actions workflow in the repository (https://github.com/DrEniolaGrace/Merros-Health)
Azure Container AppsThe web application, a database migration job and the nightly ClinVar job, from a container registry
Azure Database for PostgreSQLFlexible Server, in region
Azure Key VaultEncryption master key, signing keys and other secrets, read through a managed identity
Azure AI SpeechNatural Gulf Arabic and English voices for the Copilot
Microsoft FoundryHosting the Copilot's language model in the same region; the model provider is a configuration setting
MonitoringLog Analytics and Application Insights

Status, stated plainly. The Azure deployment is prepared but not yet live. Every service that depends on Azure has a working fallback today (browser speech, a simulated video room, the nightly job on the current host, precomputed Risk Beyond the Panel results), selected by feature flags, so switching to Azure changes configuration, not code.

Scaling

The application is stateless and runs in containers, so it scales horizontally. Couple matching runs on the users' own devices, and the risk engine is pure computation with no external calls, so neither becomes a bottleneck as use grows.

8Limitations

  • Synthetic data only. All people, clinicians, clinics, appointments and lab results are fictional. Variants are real ClinVar records assigned to fictional people.
  • Content pending accredited review. Explanations, Teach Back items, options and consent wording must be reviewed by accredited clinicians, genetic counsellors and Arabic language reviewers before real use.
  • Simulated integrations. Lab delivery, appointment booking, the counsellor directory, video sessions and sign in (a persona picker instead of UAE Pass) are simulated.
  • Gene panel. The DoH panel (September 2024) covers autosomal recessive genes. Seven conditions have written explanations; for other panel genes the couple sees the gene name and the engine's numbers, and a counsellor explains the condition.
  • Risk engine scope. Autosomal recessive couple risk only; other inheritance patterns are routed to a counsellor. Residual risk after a negative result is explained in words, not computed.
  • Blind Couple Match. The server relays blinded points only, but still stores each person's encrypted result at rest. A modified app could submit a small number of chosen genes; this is documented in the threat model.
  • Risk Beyond the Panel runs on precomputed synthetic data; there is no genomics pipeline yet.
  • Care pathway. Merros follows the DoH steps but does not issue the Premarital Screening and Counselling Report.
  • Hosting. The live prototype is hosted outside the UAE and holds synthetic data only; the UAE North deployment is prepared, not yet live.
  • Public demonstration. Anyone with the link can sign in as any synthetic persona; there is no real authentication.
  • User testing. Twelve participants, simulated scenario (section 6).

9Pilot plan

A three month pilot in two Abu Dhabi screening centres, after accredited review of all content.

Design

  • Setting: two premarital screening centres, Arabic and English speakers, a range of reading levels.
  • Participants: consenting couples receiving premarital screening results, with their genetic counsellors.
  • Integration: signed results from the screening laboratory; referral routes to the genetic counselling services listed in Appendix 13 of the DoH standard, subject to agreement from those institutions and the Department.
  • Hosting: Azure UAE North, with real authentication.

Measures

OutcomeMeasure
UnderstandingTeach Back correct before and after re-explanation, by language and reading level
Access to counsellingTime from result to results counselling; share of couples counselled together
Programme stepsTime to the Premarital Screening and Counselling Report; referrals made
Counsellor workloadPreparation time per session with the pre-session brief and Counsellor Copilot
WellbeingDistress scores and callbacks
SafetySafety Verifier blocks, escalations and their resolution
ExperienceUsability and trust, in Arabic and English

What success is not

The pilot never measures cancelled marriages or any single personal decision. Success is understanding, timely counselling and confident personal choice.

Before the pilot

  • Accredited clinical, genetic counselling and Arabic language review of all content.
  • Ethics approval and a data protection impact assessment.
  • Switching on the Azure UAE North deployment and UAE Pass sign in.

10How to run it

Use the live prototype

  1. Open https://merros-health.vercel.app. A short landing page offers two ways in.
  2. Start the Judge Tour (4 minutes) signs in as Mariam and starts the guided walk directly. Explore on your own opens the persona picker: choose anyone (for example Mariam); no password is needed, all data is synthetic.
  3. The tour is also available from the Judge Tour button in the bottom corner of every screen: six steps, following Mariam and Khalid from result day to a year later. Buttons marked Presenter control appear only during the tour; they simulate things that normally take months or two phones (switching to the partner's phone, a ClinVar update).
  4. Switch between Arabic and English at the top of any screen.

Run it locally (for technical reviewers)

Source code: https://github.com/DrEniolaGrace/Merros-Health

  1. Install Docker Desktop (https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop) and start it. Install Node.js 20 or later (https://nodejs.org) and Git.
  2. Download the code and go into its folder: 
    git clone https://github.com/DrEniolaGrace/Merros-Health.git
cd Merros-Health
  3. Create the local settings file. This generates fresh encryption and signing keys for your machine only; no account or API key is needed: 
    npm install -g pnpm@9.15.0
pnpm install
pnpm setup:env
  4. Start everything: 
    docker compose up --build
    This starts PostgreSQL, applies the database schema, loads the synthetic demo data and builds the app. The first build takes a few minutes.
  5. Open http://localhost:3000 and choose a persona.

Without an AI key the Copilot uses a built in offline model that composes answers only from approved content, so every screen works without external services.

Tests

pnpm test        # unit tests: risk engine, couple match, consent, escalation, Safety Verifier, privacy
pnpm test:e2e    # browser tests of the main journeys in Arabic and English

pnpm demo        # records the story with a browser and assembles the video (under 3 minutes)

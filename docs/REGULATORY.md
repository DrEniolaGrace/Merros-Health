# Regulatory alignment

Design intent, **subject to legal review**. Meros works alongside licensed screening and counselling services and never replaces them.

* **Federal Decree Law No. 49 of 2023 (human genome):** explicit consent before results are shown; confidentiality of genetic data (field encryption, consent gated access, audit); right to know and right to withdraw (consent revocation, choosing not to see results, opt out of reclassification recontact).
* **UAE personal data protection law:** genetic and health data treated as sensitive; explicit, purpose specific consent; purpose limitation encoded in each consent record; security by design (encryption, signatures, tamper evident audit); access, export and deletion rights in the Privacy screen.
* **Premarital screening:** results are confidential and disclosed to couples only with both partners' consent; only shared genes are revealed; couples may still marry after informed counselling, and Meros never advises on marriage decisions.
* **Data residency:** production in a UAE cloud region (Azure UAE North).
* **Clinical content:** labelled Education or Decision Support; Clinical Advice only from named accredited humans.
* **Donor gametes:** the Options Explorer states that use is restricted by UAE law and directs questions to a counsellor (to be verified by legal review).
